# SheetCut Pro
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User specified one self-contained index.html, native HTML/CSS/JavaScript, offline file://, no dependencies.
## Users
Desktop fabrication operators preparing rectangular sheet cutting jobs for metal, wood, acrylic and similar materials.
## Product Purpose
Enter stock and parts, compute geometrically valid layouts, inspect material and cutting estimates, save editable jobs and export reviewable geometry and reports.
## Brand Commitments
User specified SheetCut Pro, a compact professional CAD-style dark graphite/navy interface, optional light theme, precise dimensions and restrained motion.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Prioritize geometry correctness, finite inventory, material and thickness isolation, rotation and grain restrictions. Heuristic solutions are best found, never claimed globally optimal. SVG exports are design geometry, not machine toolpaths.
## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied a detailed functional specification. Example jobs are synthetic and labeled. No client imagery is needed.
## Product Principles
Validate before displaying; preserve physical dimensions across unit changes; expose assumptions; keep browser responsive; never invent manufacturing measurements.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, high contrast, responsive mobile workspace navigation and reduced motion.
